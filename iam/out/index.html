About IAM United

A Michigan nonprofit corporation, recognized as tax exempt under Section 501(c)(3), dedicated to preserving and celebrating Indian culture.

🎭

Our Vision

To celebrate at least one cultural event from every region of India, representing unity in diversity with board members from all regions.

🤝

Our Purpose

Bringing together families to connect the next generation with our roots while ensuring fun and cultural enrichment for all ages.

🌟

Our Roots

Founded by diverse leadership from different parts of India, bringing warmth, hospitality, and cultural pride into our shared vision.

What IAM Represents

🇮🇳
I – India:

Celebrating our heritage, traditions, and festivals.

🇺🇸
A – America:

Embracing diversity and unity in our adopted homeland.

🌊
M – Michigan:

Building a vibrant community and lasting friendships.

IAM is more than an association—it's a bridge between our roots and our future.

Our Journey Through Time

From humble beginnings to a thriving community organization, discover how IAM has grown to serve Michigan's Indian diaspora.

2003

Humble Beginnings

Two families began small gatherings to connect the next generation with our roots

2004-2006

Growing Community

Annual events held in homes, growing with community support

2007

IAM Identity

IAM name and symbolic logo created representing unity through three flags

2014-2015

Official Recognition

Social media presence launched, registered in Michigan as nonprofit organization

2016

Membership Era

Annual membership introduced, expanded events including major festivals

2025

New Leadership

First President Kumar Arbind appointed, renewed vision and board formation

Looking Forward

With renewed leadership and a clear vision, IAM is ready to embrace the future while honoring our rich past. Join us as we write the next chapter of our story.
